Calibrate on a clear highway-style lane clip (separate from Detect). Then Detect shows class and estimated distance when calibration is active.

Distance uses a pinhole camera model on a flat road. Default FOV is 120° (typical forward dashcam; editable). Lane calibration needs stable white/yellow lane paint — not guardrails or curbs. Calibration is kept for this browser session only. Not for braking, steering, or safety claims. Wide-angle lens distortion is not corrected; curves and hills reduce accuracy.
